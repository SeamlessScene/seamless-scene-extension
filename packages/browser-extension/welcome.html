Welcome to Seamless Scene

Three steps, then pick an edit and press play.

  1. 1

    Connect your account

    Connect the extension to your Seamless Scene account so it can open the edits you choose.

  2. 2

    Allow Prime Video

    The extension only works on sites you allow. It never reads your subscription or signs in for you.

  3. 3

    Choose an edit

    On an edit's page press Watch this edit. The title opens, the edit downloads, and it turns on when you press play.
