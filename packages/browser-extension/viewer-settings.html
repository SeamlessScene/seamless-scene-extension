      What the timeline shows

      The timeline is a thin bar at the right edge of the video: pale green marks a replaced scene, dark green an added scene, stripes a muted stretch and a grey line a cut. The marker shows where you are.

      Creator

      Creator mode adds Creator tools for marking scenes. Publishing on the website needs separate creator approval.

      Open a Prime Video title first; Creator tools open beside it.

      Make a .ss file

      Creator tools keep your marked scenes, not your finished clips, so the extension cannot pack an edit by itself. In the website builder, open your project and use Private playback preview: it saves a .ss file. Add that file in Settings → Local to watch it; its Export button there saves a copy again.

      Local

      Local edits. Watch edits saved as single .ss files or edit packs (.zip), from your own computer or shared with you by someone else. They play exactly like edits from the Seamless Scene catalog. Each file is checked once and kept in the extension’s storage, so you can move or delete the original. Edits you add here show as “Local: …” when you watch the matching episode.
